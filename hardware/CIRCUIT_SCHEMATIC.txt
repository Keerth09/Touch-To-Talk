===============================================================================
HARDWARE SCHEMATIC & CIRCUIT WIRING DIAGRAM
===============================================================================

1. POWER RAIL CONNECTIONS
   - Arduino 5V Pin  --> VCC Rail (+5V)
   - Arduino GND Pin --> Common Ground Rail (GND)

2. TOUCH SENSORS (TTP223)
   - Touch Sensor 1 VCC --> +5V Rail | GND --> GND Rail | OUT --> Pin D2
   - Touch Sensor 2 VCC --> +5V Rail | GND --> GND Rail | OUT --> Pin D3
   - Touch Sensor 3 VCC --> +5V Rail | GND --> GND Rail | OUT --> Pin D4

3. PULSE HEARTBEAT SENSOR
   - VCC --> +5V Rail | GND --> GND Rail | Signal (S) --> Pin A0

4. LCD DISPLAY WITH I2C BACKPACK
   - VCC --> +5V Rail | GND --> GND Rail | SDA --> Pin A4 | SCL --> Pin A5

5. APR33A3 VOICE MODULE & SPEAKER
   - VCC --> +5V Rail | GND --> GND Rail
   - M1 Trigger Pin --> Pin D5
   - M2 Trigger Pin --> Pin D6
   - M3 Trigger Pin --> Pin D7
   - Speaker Output (+/-) --> Connected directly to 8 Ohm Audio Speaker

6. BUZZER ALERT SYSTEM
   - Positive (+) Terminal --> Pin D8
   - Negative (-) Terminal --> GND Rail
